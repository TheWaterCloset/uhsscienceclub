Coaching

Interested in enhancing your leadership and communication skills while sharing a love for science? Fill out this TBD form by TBD. We'll reach out to all selected coaches once the form closes. Anyone interested is welcome to apply (you do not need to be a part of the Uni Science Olympiad team), but prior experience in science related activities is recommended. Coaches are also able to earn volunteer hours for the time they spend preparing and meeting with their students.
