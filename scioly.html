Science Olympiad (Division C)

Science Olympiad is one of the largest nation-wide STEM competitions for high school teams in the US. Formatted similarly to a track meet, Science Olympiad features 23 topics, or “events”, that include a rotation of different science- and engineering-based challenges. These events extend beyond typical classroom curriculum, covering topics such as epidemiology, astronomy, and cryptography, and many more. From conducting experiments and building robots to solving physics problems and cracking codes, SciOly offers a unique experience for everyone.

Our Science Olympiad team at University High School has consistently been one of the strongest in the state. Our team has been active for over 20 years, with many recent successes. Since 2016, we have placed within the top 10 teams at the Southern California State Tournament, most recently placing 2nd overall during the 2025-2026 season.

That said, UHS Science Olympiad is more than a list of accomplishments. What makes UHS SciOly such a special experience is that we don’t just perform well at competitions, but we make memories while doing so. We work hard, place well, and earn medals, but we also make jokes, play games, and eat really good food. We study hard and crash out harder, then cheer our throats dry at awards ceremonies. Occasionally, we pile into a plane for what we call our favorite memory of the year, and what our captains call their biggest stressor of the season—travel invitationals.

If you’re interested in becoming part of UHS Science Olympiad, join our email list to receive regular updates about upcoming events, including interest meetings and tryouts. For more information on tryouts, see our “Tryouts 2026” page.
