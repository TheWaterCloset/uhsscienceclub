Tryouts 2026

Science Olympiad

Tryout Form

Tryouts occur in mid-September (more information sent out later). We accept around 30 students across all four grades. Tryouts will test your knowledge in specific events (it is NOT a general entrance exam), so make sure you target your studying or designing/building by event; we recommend trying out for 2-4 events. Returners from last year who are not board members are required to try out as well.

The four types of events in Science Olympiad are: study, construction, hybrid, and inquiry. Study events have a written portion/test only. Construction events require competitors to build a device. Hybrid events have both a written portion and a build device or hands-on laboratory portion. Inquiry events do not fall in the other categories as they usually have a very specific format unique to them. If you want to begin studying for the tryouts this fall, scioly.org great place to start. We have general and event-specific resources compiled HERE. We highly recommend using these resources and studying consistently over the summer because Science Olympiad is something that takes time to understand and prepare for. Don't worry if Science Olympiad is intimidating at first, you will reap the benefits of your hard work!
