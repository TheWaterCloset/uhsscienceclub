Our Mission

The objective of Science Club is to enhance STEM education through team-based academic competitions, which require traditional and hands-on learning, research, experimentation, and construction. Through these competitions, students challenge and deepen both their raw knowledge in the sciences and their abilities to apply their skills into new fields and subjects.

SCIENCE OLYMPIAD

From constructing engineering devices to solving physics problems, Science Olympiad offers a variety of science- and engineering-based challenges.

SCIENCE BOWL

Teams of 5 compete in a fast-paced, buzzer-style competition testing students' knowledge of math, physics, chemistry, biology, earth and space science, and energy.
