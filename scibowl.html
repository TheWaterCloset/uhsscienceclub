Science Bowl

Science Bowl is a competition like no other. No, literally - that's the slogan for the annual National Science Bowl competition. It's a buzzer-style elimination competition where teams of five students from different high schools compete against one another in the fields of Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth and Space Science, and Energy. Invitationals happen throughout the year at campuses such as MIT and UC Berkeley, culminating in a Regionals tournament and Nationals for the top-scoring Regional teams.

University High School has historically been one of the strongest teams in the nation, consistently winning our Regionals tournament and making it to Nationals with an all-expenses-paid trip to Washington DC. In the 2025-26 season, we placed 8th at Nationals, winning $5500 for Uni!

If you're interested in joining UHS Science Bowl, join our email list to receive regular updates about upcoming events, including interest meetings and tryouts. For more information on tryouts, see our "Tryouts" page.
