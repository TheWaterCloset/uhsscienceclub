Sponsor Us

Our SciOly and Science Bowl teams are some of the strongest in California and the nation. However, each competition comes with registration, transportation, food, equipment, and sometimes lodging expenses. We understand the impact a positive STEM experience has on high schoolers, and we hope to enable each of our students to succeed by providing access to competitions and resources. As such, we would be grateful for any monetary contributions.

Because any donations would be given to University High School Science Club, they are all federal tax-deductible. Further funding information and our tiering process for sponsors are available by reaching out to us at scienceclub.uhs@gmail.com
